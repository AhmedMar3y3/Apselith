# Earth imagery

`earth-blue-marble.jpg` is NASA Earth Observatory's Blue Marble: Next Generation, July 2004 base-map composite, 5400 × 2700 pixels (8 km/pixel). It is a historical geographic texture, not live weather or current satellite imagery.

Source collection: https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/blue-marble-next-generation/base-map/

Source file: https://assets.science.nasa.gov/content/dam/science/esd/eo/images/bmng/bmng-base/july/world.200407.3x5400x2700.jpg

Credit: NASA Earth Observatory, Blue Marble: Next Generation, produced by Reto Stöckli, NASA Goddard Space Flight Center. Downloaded unchanged on 2026-10-20. No NASA endorsement is implied.

## Stars and planetary models

The Tycho star-map cubemap is distributed with CesiumJS under `Assets/Textures/SkyBox/tycho2t3_80_*.jpg`; retain Cesium and bundled third-party license notices. The Solar System diagram uses one face only as illustrative background, not a sky chart. Astronomy Engine supplies calculated planetary positions; its MIT license is included in the installed package. Sun and Moon rendering use Cesium's own astronomical models. No live sky photography is implied.
